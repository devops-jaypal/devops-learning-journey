TYPES OF NETWOKRS
1.PAN (personal area network)
  your personal devices. Bluetooth range

2.LAN (local area network)
  Your home, your office, your campus, one building or site. private

3.MAN (metropolitan area network)
  a city or large campus multiple buildings connected. Regional

4.WAN (wide area network)
  countries, continents the entire globe. the internet is the biggest example

mobile -> wifi -> routers -> ISP -> through submarines we connect the world
