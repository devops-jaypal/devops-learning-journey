						VPN (VIRTUAL PRIVATE NETWORK)

=====================================================================================================================================

In AWS, VPN (Virtual Private Network) is a networking service used to create a secure encrypted connection between your AWS VPC and another network, such as your company office, data center, or another VPC.

VPN is a secure tunnel over the internet between the two private networks

 Company Office
  10.10.0.0/16
      |
      | Internet
      |
   🔐 VPN Tunnel
      |
      |
   AWS VPC
 10.0.0.0/16

Your office employees can securely access private AWS resources:

Office PC
   |
   | VPN
   ↓
AWS VPC
   |
   ├── Private EC2
   ├── RDS
   └── Internal ALB

The traffic travels through an encrypted VPN tunnel.

=====================================================================================================================================

AWS VPC TYPES

1.site-to-site VPN

On-premises network ↔ AWS VPC

              Internet
                 |
        🔐 Site-to-Site VPN
                 |
        ┌────────┴────────┐
        │                 │
 Company Office        AWS VPC
10.10.0.0/16          10.0.0.0/16
        │                 │
   Servers/PCs          EC2/RDS

Important components of aws site-to-site vpn
  1.virtual private gateway
  2.customer gateway
  3.vpn connection
